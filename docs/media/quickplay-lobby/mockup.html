Design notes — same page, four additions
  1. Presence pill in the rules strip. Right end of the existing strip: "7 here · 3 looking" with a stacked avatar row. It's the cheapest possible "is anyone here?" answer and it sits on the line people already read for FORMAT / TIMER.
  2. "Here now" ribbon between the roster header and the tiles. One row, horizontally scrollable, ~48px. Gold chips are open lobbies (today's LIST_LOBBIES rows) with an inline Join; dim chips are people on the page with a 👋 wave; a dashed tail counts tables in play. The roster loses ~60px of height and nothing else moves.
  3. 2× XP lives in two places. The skewed gold tag at the end of the ribbon (with "ends Sun") and on the Quick Match button itself. No banner, no hero card.
  4. Quick Match keeps its slot under Create but earns its keep. Gold outline + glow (today's "armed" style, always on during the bonus), a live subline ("3 looking · ~40s"), and the 2× tag. Create stays the gold primary. The summary line under both says which one pays the bonus.
  5. Roster tiles get a tiny pip when someone in the lobby is playing that hero (the avatar) or when it's your last pick ("LAST"). Lets the roster itself hint at who's around without a new panel.
  6. Searching / found replace only the two buttons in the dock. Splash, stage, plates, roster all stay interactive — so while you wait you can still browse heroes, or just click Join on a chip in the ribbon. On match found the P2 plate fills with the opponent's hero.
