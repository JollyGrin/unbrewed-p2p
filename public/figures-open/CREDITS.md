# Credits — open-licence hero miniatures

The images in this folder are renders of the 3D models below, made by
`scripts/figures/render.cjs --open` (tinted per seat, lit and framed for the
tabletop view). The app shows the same credit in each hero's info
(`FigureCredits`), read from `manifest.json`.

| Renders | Model | Creator | Licence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `triceratops.*.webp` | Triceratops Horridus Marsh | Smithsonian Institution | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain) | https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/triceratops-horridus-marsh-e9c507f179ed4455aac3b208c9e6c973 |
| `baba-yaga.*.webp` | Witch Minis (Witch 2, file `Witch_2.stl`) | mz4250 | [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) — these renders are shared under the same licence | https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6694128 |
| `hollow-oak.*.webp`, `hollow-oak-spice.*.webp` | Autumn Treant (Meshy AI, Hollow Oak) | JollyGrin | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain) | https://unbrewed.xyz |
| `king-taranis.*.webp`, `king-taranis-spice.*.webp` | King Taranis (Meshy AI) | JollyGrin | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain) | https://unbrewed.xyz |

Changes from the originals: rendered to 2D images at a fixed camera angle,
recoloured in a single seat tint, and (Triceratops) turned to stand on the
ground. The app shows "Rendered and recoloured for Unbrewed." with every
credit whose licence is not CC0.
